## 4. What the pilot app does

The pilot app lets active Ranch Pass members:

- **Buy ball buckets at member pricing.** You select a bucket size, confirm the price, and pay through Stripe (our payment processor). No card data is stored by OTS or BJG.
- **Receive a one-time PIN.** After a successful purchase, the pilot app obtains a PIN code from SelectPi (the operator of the kiosks at the range). You present this PIN at any kiosk at your home site to dispense the balls you bought.
- **Track your activity.** The pilot app surfaces your purchase history, balls hit, savings vs. walk-in pricing, and tier progression in the Rewards tab.

The pilot app does not provide live golf instruction, equipment rentals, food and beverage purchases, or any service other than ball-bucket purchases and stat tracking. Those remain available only at the front desk.

Because this is a pre-release pilot, the pilot app may also contain bugs, incomplete features, and behaviors that are subject to change without notice. We do not promise that any particular feature observed in the pilot will appear, work identically, or remain free in the publicly released app.
