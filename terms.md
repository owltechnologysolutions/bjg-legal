## 4. What the app does

The app lets active Ranch Pass members:

- **Buy ball buckets at member pricing.** You select a bucket size, confirm the price, and pay through Stripe (our payment processor). No card data is stored by OTS or BJG.
- **Receive a one-time PIN.** After a successful purchase, the app obtains a PIN code from SelectPi (the operator of the kiosks at the range). You present this PIN at any kiosk at your home site to dispense the balls you bought.
- **Track your activity.** The app surfaces your purchase history, balls hit, savings vs. walk-in pricing, and tier progression in the Rewards tab.

The app does not provide live golf instruction, equipment rentals, food and beverage purchases, or any service other than ball-bucket purchases and stat tracking. Those remain available only at the front desk.
